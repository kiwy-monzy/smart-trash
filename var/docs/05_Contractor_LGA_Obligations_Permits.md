# 05 - CONTRACTOR-LGA OBLIGATIONS AND ALL PERMITS (KIBALI)
## What Contractors Give To vs Receive From the LGA + Complete Licensing Path

*This document answers in full: (A) how money flows, (B) what contractors
give/pay to the LGA, (C) what contractors receive from the LGA, and
(D) every permit (kibali) needed to operate as a waste contractor.*

> **Transparency note:** the 9 downloaded reports document the contract
> mechanics (tender, duration, RCC sharing, disbursement, reporting) but do
> not contain a consolidated official licensing checklist. Section D is
> therefore the **standard Tanzanian legal pathway** built from EMA 2004 +
> GN 263/2009 (waste permits, Reg 9) + GN 387/2021 (fees) + business law -
> verify exact procedures with BRELA, TRA, NEMC and the municipality.
> All legal texts cited are now downloaded in laws-and-regulations/ and

## A. THE MONEY FLOW - STEP BY STEP

```
                    (1) PAY RCC
   HOUSEHOLD / SHOP / HOTEL / INSTITUTION
        |  - monthly: households TZS 2k-15k+ (estimate)
        |  - by cash (common) or mobile money (rare)
        ▼
   CONTRACTOR (private company)
        |
        |--(2) KEEPS ITS SHARE: 50% - 100%
        |      depending on the contract (VPO 2022, Fig 11-10)
        |
        |--(3) REMITS TO LGA: 0% - 50%
        |      (where the contract gives the contractor 100%,
        |       the LGA receives nothing in this flow)
        ▼
   MUNICIPALITY (LGA)
        |
        |--(4) keeps its oversight/administration share
        |
        |--(5) SHOULD remit its obligations back to the contractor
        |      - BUT COMMONLY AFTER 30+ DAYS (VPO 2022, Fig 11-7)
        ▼
   CONTRACTOR PAYS ITS COSTS
        |-- staff (crews) - often paid late as a knock-on effect
        |-- fuel + truck maintenance
        |-- disposal trips to Pugu (own fleet costs)
        |-- taxes + licenses (TRA, LGA, NSSF, WCF...)
```

### A.1 Worked Example (illustrative)
Contract terms: contractor share **70%**, LGA share **30%**.

| Step | Amount (example month) |
|------|------------------------|
| Contractor collects RCC from 5,000 households x TZS 5,000 avg | TZS 25,000,000 |
| Contractor keeps 70% (directly) | TZS 17,500,000 |
| Contractor remits 30% to the LGA | TZS 7,500,000 |
| Contractor costs (40 crew x 150k + fuel 4M + maintenance 2M) | ~TZS 12,000,000 |
| **Contractor margin (if the LGA paid on time)** | ~TZS 5,500,000 |
| **Reality: LGA pays after 30+ days** -> contractor carries credit strain, cuts truck days, crews paid late | **Margin erodes -> service degrades** |

**Takeaway:** because the contractor collects money directly but depends on
the LGA for disbursements, the flow creates a structural cash conflict. This
is exactly why the Smart System (doc 06) proposes **automatic mobile-money
revenue split** - funds divide instantly at payment time, with no waiting.

## B. WHAT THE CONTRACTOR GIVES TO THE LGA

| # | Item | Details | Source |
|---|------|---------|--------|
| **1** | **RCC revenue share** | The contractor remits the agreed non-contractor share (0-50% depending on the contract) | VPO 2022 Fig 11-10 |
| **2** | **Performance reports** | GN 263/2009 Reg 60: permit holders keep records of the licensed activity and submit them to the LGA **every 6 months** (LGA forwards to NEMC); Reg 53 requires LGA annual reports | GN 263/2009 Regs 53+60 (PDF in laws-and-regulations/); REPOA 2024 |
| **3** | **The service itself (in-kind)** | The core deliverable: household collection (1-3x/week), haulage to Pugu, cleaning of collection points | WB PID 2023 |
| **4** | **Tender/bid fees** | Procurement participation fees during tendering | Standard practice |
| **5** | **Business license** | Annual municipal business license for the waste-sanitation category | Standard practice |
| **6** | **Taxes** | VAT + income tax (TRA); municipal service levy | Standard practice |
| **7** | **Staff contributions** | NSSF + WCF for employees (labour law) | Standard practice |

## C. WHAT THE CONTRACTOR RECEIVES FROM THE LGA

| # | Item | Details |
|---|------|---------|
| **1** | **THE PERMIT = the exclusive franchise contract** | The principal "kibali": the LGA grants the contractor the **exclusive right** to collect waste in its zone for 6 months - 3 years. No other contractor may serve that zone | WB PID 2023; VPO 2022 |
| **2** | **RCC revenue share** | 50-100% of all charges collected in the zone | VPO 2022 Fig 11-10 |
| **3** | **Legal backing** | The council can enforce payment through by-laws (fines, enforcement lists, ward courts) - a power the contractor does not have alone | EMA 2004; by-laws |
| **4** | **Public sensitization** | LGA/ward committees educate residents about RCC and the service - raising compliance for the contractor | Ward committees |
| **5** | **(Future) Equipment support** | DMDP2 Component 2 plans "equipment and facilities to support solid waste collection and cleaning services" for the DLAs - which can flow through to contractors | WB PID 2023 (Component 2 item iv) |

## D. ALL PERMITS (KIBALI) NEEDED TO BECOME A WASTE CONTRACTOR

### D.1 Company-Level (before tendering)
| Permit/Step | Issued by | Purpose |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| **1. Company registration** | BRELA | Certificate of incorporation |
| **2. TRA registration** | TRA | TIN; VAT registration (above threshold); tax compliance |
| **3. Business license** | Municipality/Ministry | License category covering waste collection & sanitation services |
| **4. Environmental permit** | LGA issues the waste permit (GN 263/2009 Reg 9); NEMC role via EMA 2004 + fees per GN 387/2021 | Waste collection/transport/disposal/transfer-station permit - the environmental licence to handle waste. Application needs: fee + proof of means/facilities + activity description (Reg 9(4)) |
| **5. NSSF + WCF registration** | NSSF / WCF | Social security and workers' compensation for staff |
| **6. Financial capacity evidence** | Bank statements, asset registers | Required at tender evaluation |

### D.2 Contract-Level (through the tender)
| Permit/Step | Issued by |
|-------------|-----------|
| **7. Winning the tender** (bid submission + evaluation) | LGA procurement (Public Procurement Act) |
| **8. Contract / Franchise agreement** (the exclusive permit) | Signed with the LGA - contains duration (6mo-3yr), KPIs, RCC sharing % |
| **9. Performance bond / bank guarantee** (commonly required) | Guarantees contract execution |
| **10. Signed zone map + service schedule** | Annex to the contract |

### D.3 Operational-Level (during service)
| Permit/Step | Issued by |
|-------------|-----------|
| **11. Vehicle inspection certificates** | TRA/TARURA - waste vehicles must meet hygiene standards |
| **12. Records every 6 months to the LGA** | To the LGA, which forwards to NEMC (GN 263/2009 Reg 60) |
| **13. Safety/PPE compliance** | OSHA - REPOA 2024 showed 50% of workers untrained; this is a legal requirement that goes unenforced |
| **14. Disposal/gate fee payments at Pugu** | Per-load fees at the dumpsite (standard practice) |

### D.4 For CBOs and Small Collectors
- Street-level CBOs typically hold **simplified agreements with the ward/mtaa
  leadership** (not full tenders), supervised by the ward environmental
  committee.
- **Formalization plan (VPO 2022):** the Master Plan targets mapping and
  formalizing informal collectors - mapping by 2025.
- Pugu waste pickers: DMDP2's livelihoods program plans to absorb them into
  the formal system (WB PID 2023).

## E. SUMMARY (TL;DR)

1. **Money flow:** Household -> contractor (collects RCC directly) ->
   contractor keeps 50-100% -> remits 0-50% to the LGA -> LGA should remit
   its obligations back but delays 30+ days -> contractor pays staff/fuel/
   fleets. **The delay is the structural failure.**
2. **Contractor gives to the LGA:** RCC share, the service itself, performance
   reports, business license + taxes, tender fees.
3. **Contractor receives from the LGA:** the **exclusive franchise (the
   principal permit)**, the 50-100% RCC share, legal enforcement backing,
   public sensitization, and (planned) equipment support.
4. **Permits to enter the business:** BRELA company -> TRA TIN/VAT ->
   business license -> waste permit from the LGA (GN 263/2009 Reg 9; fees
   per GN 387/2021) -> NSSF/WCF -> win the tender -> sign the franchise contract ->
   performance bond -> operational compliance (vehicle certs, 6-monthly records,
   PPE/OSHA, Pugu gate fees).
