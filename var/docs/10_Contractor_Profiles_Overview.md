## B. THE MONEY-FLOW MODELS THE CONTRACTORS OPERATE UNDER (real)

**Model 1 - "Collect and keep a share" (Kinondoni/Ubungo standard):**
Household pays RCC -> CONTRACTOR KEEPS 50-100% -> remits 0-50% to council
(VPO Master Plan 2022 Fig 11-10; Ubungo variant: money flows through mtaa executive officers).

**Model 2 - "Collect and deposit all" (Ilala/DCC):**
Household pays RCC -> CONTRACTOR DEPOSITS 100% into the council account -> council deducts
the contractual commission (often ~10% of the tender amount) -> remits the remainder
(Ilomo & Charles 2025, quoting Ilala's municipal environmental officer).

**Model 3 (emerging, Kinondoni 2024):** council PAYS providers per verified service
(tender 108016059) - results-based logic from the World Bank RBF knowledge paper (doc 08 Part H).

**Model 4 (documented sub-ward):** ward contracts user-fee collection to grassroots groups
(Magomeni Taka, Mandela, Vingunguti, Peramiho, Bunju, Mbagala's three groups).

### C.1 What the CONTRACTOR CHARGES citizens (RCC)

RCC introduced **1993** (DCC by-laws), adopted by the municipalities 2000-01; typical
residential band **TZS 1,150-2,300/month** (CSE 2021) with 30-50% payment compliance in
low-income areas; market band: households TZS 2,000-15,000; shops 10,000-30,000;
hotels 50,000-300,000. **Documented real tariffs:** Taka Ajira bids 4,000/8,000/15,000
(Kigamboni 2022); Temeke refuse fees collected FY2022/23 = **TZS 3,790,711,379** (CAG App 46,
p.355); Ilala model commission ~10% of tender amount.
